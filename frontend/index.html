    How it works

    1. 1
      Set your stop

      Search a place, tap the map, or paste a Google Maps link.

    2. 2
      Start the trip

      The Java server fetches the real road route and follows your bus along it, live.

    3. 3
      Sleep

      A loud alarm, vibration and a notification wake you before you get there.

    Why it's accurate

    • Road distance, not a straight line. GPS fixes are map-matched onto the route — safely through U-turns and roads you use twice.
    • ETA from your bus's real pace, including signals and stops, reacting within ~2 minutes when it speeds up.
    • Re-routes automatically if the driver takes another road.
    • Dead-zone safety net: no GPS or no internet? It keeps counting down and leans early, never late.
    • Java engine, tested on 600+ simulated rides on the real Hyderabad → Warangal road: with normal GPS it rings within a few dozen metres of your point.

    Questions

    Does it work with the screen off?

    Phones pause websites when locked, so during a trip the app keeps your screen awake. Night mode turns it almost black to save battery.

    Does it need internet?

    Yes to start a trip and to follow it on the server. In dead zones your phone keeps its own estimate and can still ring.

    Is my location shared?

    Only with the Hibernate Express server for the length of your trip, to follow it on the road route. No account, and trips are deleted when they end.

    Can I use it on trains?

    Yes — choose Train. It uses the straight-line distance to your station, so the alarm rings a little early.

    iPhone tips

    Share → Add to Home Screen for notifications. The alarm plays even with the silent switch on; keep the media volume up.
